An agency competitor record is what we know about
a business that is competing with us for the
same customers.
We have one right now.
This object exists because comparison pages are one on
facts.
If we publish a page saying we are better
than somebody, and we cannot back it up, that
page is marketing opinion, and it will not survive
contact with a skeptical buyer.
So this record is deliberately a fact base, not
an opinion store.
It is where we keep what we can actually
verify about arrival, so that when we write the
comparison, every claim has a source behind it.
The fields are mostly identifiers and measurements.
The name is the company.
The domain name and the website are the addresses
we analyze.
The industry places them against us.
Competitive level is our assessment of how directly they
compete, whether they are in our market and our
geography or merely adjacent.
Then ranked keywords, which is the most valuable field
on the object because it is the list of
searches this competitor already ranks for.
And finally, a go to market priority, which is
our own ranking of whether it is worth writing
about them all, because there is no point researching
a competitor we will never mention.
Why does the ranked keywords field matter so much?
Because a competitor's keyword list is a free, honest
description of what their business is actually selling.
Not what they say on their website, which is
aspirational, but what customers are searching for and finding
them for.
If they rank for cheap window tinting in leads,
that is what their revenue looks like.
Also tells us where they are weak, because the
searches that appear on our list and not theirs
are the openings.
This single field turns competitive research from a guessing
game into a comparison.
Where does this get used in practice?
It is a pre flight check.
Before anybody writes a comparison page, they open this
record and every claim in the draft must map
to a field in here.
If the claim is not supported, it does not
get published.
That single discipline is what stops us from saying
something embarrassing about a competitor.
The record is also what feeds the agency content
object, since comparison pages are created and tracked there
like any other page.
This object is small and deliberately boring, and it
is what will let us compete on facts once
it is not.
So here is the practical end of this object.
You have one competitor row, and it has a
ranked keyword list on it.
Read that list as a set of questions people
are already typing.
Every phrase in there is a person at a
keyboard who has a problem and is comparing options.
That list is the outline for a comparison page.
Not the features we happen to like, not the
things we are proudest of, but the things people
are actively searching for in the order they search
for them.
And the priority field tells you which of those
phrases is worth a page of its own, versus
a paragraph.
The competitive level tells you how hard the fight
is, which decides whether you write something genuinely useful
or simply accurate.
The industry field is the sanity check.
If a record claims to be a competitor in
an industry we do not work in, it is
a data error and it will quietly poison whatever
you build on top of it.
So verify the row before you write a word.
