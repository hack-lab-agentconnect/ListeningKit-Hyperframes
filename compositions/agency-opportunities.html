An agency opportunity is a specific commercial deal that
is in flight.
Right now there are zero, and that is not
a bug.
That is just where we are honestly at.
An opportunity is the moment a lead stops being
a conversation and becomes a priced, staged, owned piece
of work.
It sits on top of a lead, always.
It cannot exist on its own because an opportunity
without a person to sell to and a company
to sell for is just a number in a
spreadsheet.
If somebody asks you why the count is zero,
that is a fact about where the business is,
not a data problem.
There are only four fields on an opportunity, and
every one of them earns its place.
The name is the deal's identity, usually the client
name and the package name.
The stage is where it sits in the pipeline,
and this is the field that actually drives forecasting.
The description is the short written summary of what
is being sold, and at what price.
And the owner is the single most important field
on the object.
An opportunity without a named owner is a deal
nobody is chasing, and those quietly die.
The owner is who picks up the phone.
Why build a separate object for this rather than
just a status on the lead?
Because it separates activity from money.
Everything else in this library is activity.
Prospects were found.
Leads were rung.
Calls were placed.
Scripts were read.
None of that is revenue.
An opportunity is the first object in the system
that represents money we might actually receive.
That is why it gets its own stage field,
its own owner field, and its own record.
It is also why the stage field has to
be kept honest, because every forecast anybody gives is
a rollup of opportunity stages.
So where will this object get used once it
is populated?
In two places.
The first is forecasting, which is simply grouping opportunities
by stage to answer the question how much money
is coming and when.
The second is the handover.
When a client agrees to buy, somebody creates the
opportunity off the agency offer object, sets the stage,
and assigns the owner.
It is the moment the sales conversation becomes a
commitment.
And because it is empty right now, it is
worth understanding honestly.
Zero opportunities with 18 leads means the motion is
running, but the closing conversation has not landed yet.
