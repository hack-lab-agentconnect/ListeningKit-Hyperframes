An agency task is an internal to do item,
and the important word is attached.
A task is not a floating note in a
list somewhere.
It hangs directly off a prospect, a lead, an
opportunity, an application, any of the other objects in
this library.
There are zero tasks at the moment, which again
tells you something honest about where the team is.
But the design is the thing worth understanding because
it is the difference between a business that follows
up and a business that means two.
Three fields, and all three are load bearing.
The title is what the work is, written so
that somebody who was not in the room understands
it in two seconds.
The status tells you whether it is still to
do, in progress, blocked, or done.
And that is what lets anybody glance at a
record and see the real real state of it.
The assignee is the named human who is doing
it.
Drop the assignee, and you have a wish.
Drop the status, and you have a note.
With all three, you have a commitment with a
name on it.
Why insist that follow-up work lives next to the
record rather than in a separate list?
Because separate lists are where work goes to die.
Somebody writes call this shop back on Thursday in
a notes app.
Thursday arrives.
Nobody can remember which shop.
The deal goes cold and nobody can even explain
why.
When the task hangs off the prospect record, then
opening the prospect tells you exactly what still needs
doing and who owns it.
You cannot look at a live deal without seeing
the outstanding work attached to it.
So where does this get used day to day?
In the five second check before anything is dropped.
A call goes badly, so a task appears on
the lead to call back Thursday.
An advert goes live, so a task appears on
the content record to check the rankings in two
weeks.
An application comes in, so a task appears to
reply.
That is it.
That is the entire life cycle.
There are no tasks at the moment, which I
would read as an opportunity rather than a problem,
because the first genuinely useful thing anybody does in
a system like this is a task a task
to the record they were already looking at.
Then the pipeline starts telling the truth.
