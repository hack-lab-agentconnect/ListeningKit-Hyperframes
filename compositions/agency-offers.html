An agency offer is a sellable package we offer
to a client.
We have one at the moment, and that is
because we are still working out what the product
actually is, which is normal and healthy at this
stage.
This object is the commercial center of the whole
system.
Everything else exists to help us sell one of
these.
A prospect is somebody we might sell it to.
A lead is somebody we are talking to about
it.
A call is where we pitch it.
An opportunity is where they agree to buy it.
Bought.
The fields tell you how an offer reaches a
human.
There is the name and the brand name, so
we know whose product this is.
Then, hero h one, which is the big headline
that appears at the top of the offer page.
Then the call to action type, so the system
knows whether the button should book a call or
buy or start a chat.
Then the status, which controls whether the page is
live, draft, or paused.
And the two fields that actually make money.
The Calendly URL, which is the booking link the
customer lands on, and the Metapixel ID, which is
the tracking identifier that lets us attribute an advert
all the way to a booked call.
Why hold the pitch copy and the tracking inside
a CRM record rather than in a website builder?
Because consistency, and because attribution.
If the offer page says one thing, and the
advert says another, and the sales call says a
third, the customer is being confused, and we are
losing the credit.
This record is the single source for the offer
page, the advert creative, and the calendar link.
And the pixel ID being here means we can
answer the only question that matters about marketing, which
is which advert actually produced booked calls, rather than
which advert produced clicks.
So where do you meet an offer in practice?
In three places.
On the website, where the offer page renders from
the Booking button In the advertising on the call
Having only one active offer is genuinely useful, because
it forces one sharp description of what we do.
When the second one appears, this object is where
you will model it, and the fields here tell
you exactly what you have to fill in before
you can launch it.
