An agency listing is a directory style page we
publish in order to show up for a local
search.
We have two at the moment.
The idea is a very old one and it
still works.
Somebody in Leeds searches for window tinting leads.
We want a page of ours to be the
result.
Each listing is one such page.
It is not a home page and it is
not a blog post.
It is a thin, local, highly specific page built
around a location query, and the whole point of
keeping them in the CRM is that there are
hundreds of them over time, and they need managing.
The title is the page heading.
The location query is the actual search term we
are targeting, which is the field that makes the
record meaningful, because a listing without a location query
is just a page.
Then the status, whether it is live, pending, or
being worked.
Then two fields that handle the messy reality of
SEO.
A parent listing ID and a superseded by ID.
These let us tell the story of a page
being rewritten without losing the traffic the old one
had earned.
And finally, the screenshot URL, a picture of the
live page, which sounds cosmetic and is not.
It is proof the page rendered, which is the
first thing that breaks.
Why invest in listings rather than nicer pages?
Because local intent is the cheapest repeatable win available
to us.
A good homepage might take a year to rank.
A well built location page for a town we
have never targeted can start bringing inquiries in weeks.
And because the template repeats, the cost per page
falls as we build more.
That compounding is why these live in a structured
object rather than being built ad hoc.
The downside is volume creates mess, which is exactly
what the parent and superseded fields exist to manage.
Where does a person actually use this object?
Three times.
When we need to prove a listing is live,
the screenshot URL is the receipt, and it is
faster and more reliable than asking somebody to go
and check.
When a page is rewritten, the parent and superseded
fields stop us from throwing away rankings we spent
months earning.
And when the agency contact object renders the live
website, listing pages are what it is pulling.
If you want to understand how the search side
of this business works, this is the object.
Two records today.
It is very early and it is the clearest
example of a play that scales without more headcount.
