# ears

The ListeningKit brand's die-cut ear cutouts, `ear1.webp` .. `ear8.webp`.

- Source: `listeningkit-hackathon/apps/web/public/images/ears/` (this team's own repository).
- Used on the landing page (`apps/web/src/landing/Header.tsx`, `SECTION_EAR_MAP`) as one sticker per section.
- Here they are the cutout satellites in the depth set (`src/components/cutout`), chosen by what a scene is about
  (`earFor` in that component). Mirrored under `compositions/assets/ears/` because sub-compositions resolve
  `assets/` relative to `compositions/`.
